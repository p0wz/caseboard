Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective Date: September 21, 2026

SUMMARY: Caseboard does not collect, transmit, share, or monetize any of your personal data. The application operates 100% offline directly on your device.

1. Overview & Core Privacy Principles

At Caseboard: Detective Mystery (developed by p0wz), we respect and protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you play our mobile application.

Our guiding philosophy is simple: Your device is your private office. Caseboard is designed from the ground up as an offline game. We do not maintain any cloud databases, user registration systems, or remote tracking servers.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

We collect ZERO personal data.

  • No Personal Identification: We do not ask for your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate third-party telemetry, crash report trackers, or advertising frameworks (such as Google Firebase, Meta SDK, or Unity Ads).
  • No Tracking: In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and App Store Guideline 5.1.1, our app declares NSPrivacyTracking = false and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. On-Device Storage & System APIs

To maintain your gameplay progress, Caseboard stores minimal game-state data strictly locally on your device:

  • Local Game Progress: Solved cold cases, pinned evidence positions, discovered clues, and unlocked audio recordings are stored using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage.
  • Apple Declared Reason (CA92.1): As declared in our official Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), UserDefaults is accessed solely for the declared purpose of reading and writing configuration and app state directly inside the application sandbox. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Caseboard complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. Because we do not collect, process, or transmit any personal data from any user, we do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. GDPR & CCPA / CPRA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Access & Portability: All game data is stored exclusively on your device. You have immediate, unfiltered access to it.
  • Right to Deletion: You can completely erase all data stored by the app by using the "Reset Progress" option in the game settings or by deleting the app from your iOS device.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
